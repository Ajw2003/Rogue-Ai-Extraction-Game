# Plan: Issue 32 - Lair lighting and mood pass - "one candle, one fire, falling into black"

## Exhaustive Outline
The goal of this task is to create the lighting for the player's home base, known as the lair. This space needs to look completely different from the dangerous areas in the game, feeling warm and safe. The only sources of light will be a single candle and a single fire, with the rest of the room fading into complete darkness. This work cannot begin until the basic 3D room for the lair has been built.

## Step by Step Execution Instructions

1.  **Check Dependencies:**
    Verify that the basic 3D space for the lair has been created in the game. Do not start lighting until this is complete.
2.  **Remove Default Lighting:**
    Open the lair scene and turn off all standard or automatic lights so the room is pitch black.
3.  **Place the Fire Light:**
    Add a single warm light source at the location of the fire. Adjust it so the light slowly fades away into the surrounding darkness.
4.  **Place the Candle Light:**
    Add a single small, warm light source at the location of the candle.
5.  **Refine the Mood:**
    Adjust the colors and brightness of both lights so the room feels safe, warm, and visually distinct from the dangerous levels. Ensure the outer edges of the room remain entirely black.

3.  Check the edges of the room to verify they fade completely into darkness.

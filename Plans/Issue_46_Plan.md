# Plan: Issue 46 - Portal has no closing-countdown feedback, "the way home begins to narrow" is invisible

## Exhaustive Outline
The escape portal currently lacks any visual or audio cues to let players know it is closing soon. The goal of this task is to add clear countdown indicators, such as changing colors or ticking sounds, so players know exactly how much time they have left to escape. This ensures the game creates the intended tension and urgency.

## Step by Step Execution Instructions

1.  **Add Visual Countdown:**
    Modify the portal appearance to change color, pulse, or shrink as the timer ticks down.
2.  **Add Audio Countdown:**
    Introduce a sound effect that plays and speeds up, or gets louder, as the portal gets closer to closing.
3.  **Make Cues Noticeable from Afar:**
    Ensure these visual and audio cues are large and loud enough to be noticed by players who are still running toward the portal from a distance.
4.  **Connect to Game Timer:**
    Link these new visual and audio cues directly to the existing portal countdown timer in the game rules.

## Verification Steps
1.  Start the game and trigger the portal to open.
2.  Stand far away from the portal and observe if you can hear and see the closing warnings.
3.  Watch the portal until it closes to confirm the visual and audio effects match the actual closing time perfectly.

## Completion Checks
*   [ ] The portal has a visible countdown effect like shrinking or changing colors.
*   [ ] The portal has an audible countdown effect like a ticking sound.
*   [ ] The countdown effects are clear from a long distance.
*   [ ] The effects sync correctly with the actual game timer.

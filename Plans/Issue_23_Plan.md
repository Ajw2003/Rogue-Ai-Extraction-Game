<Plan: Issue 23 - Castles look bland>
## Exhaustive Outline
The castle environment currently looks very flat and dull because it uses the same gray colors everywhere and lacks varied lighting. The goal of this task is to improve the visual quality of the castles by making different areas look distinct, adding varied materials, and improving the lighting and visual effects so the rooms have more depth and atmosphere.

## Step by Step Execution Instructions

1.  **Assign Materials to Zones:**
    Update the game to use different textures and colors for different castle areas (like crypts, keeps, and baileys) instead of a single gray material.
2.  **Improve Global Lighting:**
    Adjust the main sunlight settings and add softer ambient lighting to prevent the scene from looking too dark or flat.
3.  **Add Local Lights:**
    Place light sources inside the rooms (like torches or glowing altars) and ensure the player character's lantern emits light to brighten up the immediate surroundings.
4.  **Configure Visual Effects:**
    Enable camera effects (like color correction and bloom) to give the game a more polished and atmospheric look.

## Verification Steps
1.  Start a raid level in the game.
2.  Walk through different areas (like the crypt and keep) to verify they look visually distinct.
3.  Check that the lantern is casting light on the environment.
4.  Confirm that the overall scene looks more detailed and less flat compared to the previous version.

## Completion Checks
*   [ ] Different zones in the castle are visually distinguishable from one another.
*   [ ] The castle uses varied materials instead of a single gray color.
*   [ ] Lighting and visual effects provide depth to the interiors.
